# Claim Ceiling

R0 is a numerically qualified calibration baseline for the bounded recorded configuration.

It supports code-verification and numerical-qualification claims under its analytical, reduced-twin, time-step, mesh, heterogeneous-pressure, MPI, conservation, and provenance gates.

It does not establish independent real-coffee validation. Physical validation is `NOT_ESTABLISHED`. The approximately 40 g endpoint at 30 s was used in saturated-permeability calibration. The software does not yet predict taste or transfer reliably across coffees, grinders, baskets, machines, or recipes.

WP02-001 supports a narrower source-linked reconstruction claim: the optional
saturated effective-resistance closure passed its predeclared 9-bar
reconstruction and 8-bar no-retuning same-campaign comparison. The latter is
not independent validation. Neither result establishes full poroelastic
deformation, cross-rig transfer, early wetting, channeling, chemistry, taste,
or a universal permeability law.
