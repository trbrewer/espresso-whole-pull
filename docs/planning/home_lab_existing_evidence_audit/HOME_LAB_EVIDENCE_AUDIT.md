## Disposition

Accepted Puckworks evidence is sufficient to choose preliminary operating ranges, logger ranges, sample volumes, fraction boundaries, uncertainty priors, and qualification tests. It is not sufficient for a controlled Flair comparison or a paired inventory/species balance. No equipment should be purchased before a zero/low-cost Flair qualification using the existing scale, phone/camera, thermometer, vessels, and computer.

## Existing evidence to use first

- Waszkiewicz pressure/flow/mass traces and fraction TDS set conservative hydraulic, logging, and fraction signal ranges while preserving basket-versus-line pressure and scale-flow definitions.
- Schmieder/Pannusch fraction and cup chemistry informs six-fraction switching, expected early-to-late signal decline, late-fraction LOQ risk, and external-service specifications. These sources were consumed in SCI-MD-004/007/008/009 and cannot become fresh holdouts.
- Dias, Viencz, and Acre constrain coffee/species total-content priors only. Total roasted content is neither `I_ref` nor production `M0`.
- Liang informs method-conditioned equilibrium/reference-extractability and dry-basis planning only. It is not a paired production inventory.
- Waszkiewicz/Wadsworth PSD and Wadsworth/Romancorrochano porosity/permeability constrain later equipment ranges. Grinder setting is not PSD, and those rigs do not transfer quantitatively to a home grinder or Flair.
- Foster CT/front/deformation and Egidi bulk TDS/EY are method and signal context, not controlled Flair comparisons.

## Minimum missing joins

The irreducible new measurements are synchronized Flair mass/time and first drip, plus quantitative Flair pressure only if the existing gauge/video route fails qualification. The paired chemistry lane requires same-lot moisture, initial sequential `I_ref`, mass-defined fraction mass and TDS, caffeine/trigonelline concentration or mass, spent-puck `I_ref`, recovery/loss accounting, and a cup–spent-puck mass balance. External chromatography is required before instrument purchase. Later grinder work still needs grinder/preparation-specific PSD, porosity/permeability, and deformation tie-points.

## Fraction design

The provisional first design is six equal beverage-mass fractions. It matches the current pilot scale, provides straightforward absolute-mass accounting, and limits switching and late-fraction analytical burden. The fallback is early-dense mass fractions, pooling more late beverage to protect analytical signal. Both are `PROVISIONAL_PRE-PILOT_DESIGN`, `NOT_OPTIMIZED_MINIMUM`, and `SUBJECT_TO_HOME_COLLECTION_AND_ANALYTICAL_FEASIBILITY`. Eight fractions and ten source-like fractions are deferred until six-fraction collection and assay feasibility are demonstrated.
