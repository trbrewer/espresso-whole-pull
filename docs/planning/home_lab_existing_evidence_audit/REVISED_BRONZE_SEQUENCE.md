# Revised Bronze sequence

All stages preserve `PHYSICAL_VALIDATION_NOT_ESTABLISHED`.

## B-1 — Existing-evidence audit

Information: evidence coverage, missing joins, preliminary ranges and purchase gates. Uses all accepted Puckworks sources in the matrix. Equipment: none. Qualification: reference closure, privacy/static checks, deterministic regeneration. Stop if authority or rights do not close. Next gate: B0 only.

## B0 — Use-existing-equipment Flair qualification

Information: scale rate/resolution/drift, video synchronization, visible first drip/gauge, beverage mass/time, temperature repeatability, vessel/switch losses. Unique data: low-cost Flair tie-points. Equipment: existing scale, phone/camera, thermometer, vessels, computer. Pass only if timestamps, calibration comparisons, repeatability and recovery meet the later observable's declared uncertainty need. Defer purchase when adequate; otherwise advance only the failed function to B0A.

## B0A — Minimum telemetry gap closure

Information: only a failed B0 observable, most likely synchronized pressure. Uses source envelopes for range selection. Equipment: the minimum failed function; a pressure transducer is likely first if gauge/video fails. Require node identity, calibration, leak, sample-rate, synchronization and repeatability tests. Do not buy inline flow when scale-derived outlet mass flow answers the decision. Next gate: B1 after telemetry and collection feasibility.

## B1 — Fraction TDS

Information: six mass-defined Flair fraction masses/times/TDS and whole-shot closure. Existing source curves choose range and late-signal tests. Equipment: qualified existing or purchased refractometer and manual collector. Require water/standard checks, temperature/repeat precision, carryover, switch loss and summed-mass closure. Stop for inadequate late-fraction precision or recovery; then revise/pooled fallback before purchasing more apparatus.

## B2 — Dry basis and mass closure

Information: lot moisture, dry-basis dose/EY, initial/reference and spent accounting design. Equipment: use or borrow qualified drying capability; oven/desiccator only after method selection. Require repeat mass-to-criterion, blanks, recovery and a complete identifier chain. Defer until the paired inventory pilot is operationally current.

## B3/B4 — Quantitative preparation and controlled extraction

Information: method-conditioned initial/spent `I_ref`, sequential tail and recovery. Equipment: balance, pipettes/glassware and extraction controls only as demanded by selected volumes/method; external service remains preferred. Require gravimetric volume checks, blanks, spikes/recovery, temperature/time uniformity, repeat preparation and controlled tail. Stop until analytical method, volumes, uncertainty, and service requirements are fixed.

## B5 — Clarification and archive

Information: demonstrated clarification recovery, holding time and archive integrity. Equipment: tubes, filters/vials and only then centrifuge/cold storage if the workflow proves necessary. Require adsorption/recovery, carryover, clarity, stability and excursion checks. Defer generic purchases without a demonstrated workflow.

## B6 — External chemistry reference batch

Information: caffeine/trigonelline concentration and absolute mass, LOQ/censoring, calibration, recovery and paired closure. Equipment: external chromatography service first. Require standards/curves, blanks, spike recovery, dilution, LOQ, carryover, batch identity and retained native outputs. UV-Vis or HPLC purchase remains unjustified until repeated validated throughput and service economics create a separately approved need.
