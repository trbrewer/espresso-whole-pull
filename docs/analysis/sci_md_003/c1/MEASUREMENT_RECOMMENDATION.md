# C1 measurement recommendation

`NO_COMPLETE_MEASUREMENT_SET`

No synchronized package is nominated because no eligible cross-repository pair
exists under the retained cases. Establishing a genuinely matched machine case,
explicit pressure-node and flow bases, and declared uncertainties is required
before coverage can be assessed. Separate upstream and basket pressure and
approximately 0.05 mm deformation resolution remain model-informed starting
assumptions, not universal requirements or acquired evidence.
