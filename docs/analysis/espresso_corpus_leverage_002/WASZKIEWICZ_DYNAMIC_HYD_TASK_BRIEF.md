# XSV-WASZKIEWICZ-DYNAMIC-HYD-001

## Grouped comparison of fixed versus evolving basket-node effective hydraulic resistance

Question: Does fixed effective resistance adequately predict the qualified
Waszkiewicz pressure–flow–mass response across held-out physical brews and
controlled conditions, or is one bounded evolving component justified?

Authority: 56 distinct physical brews in 11 controlled conditions. Physical
brew is the experimental unit and condition is the higher grouping. Time rows
are samples, not independent observations. Retain the source's exact basket-
node pressure derived from measured line pressure by fitted brewer-loss
subtraction, scale-measured then aligned/interpolated mass, Savitzky-Golay
mass-derivative flow, synchronization/cadence, exclusions, rights, and
aggregate-SEM meaning.

Freeze a bounded subset of W-H0 fixed resistance; W-H1 monotone time-varying;
W-H2 cumulative-mass/progress-dependent; W-H3 one bounded change point; W-H4
pressure/flow-dependent; and W-H5 observation-delay alternative where source
semantics justify it. Prefer direct qualified pressure, flow, or mass prediction
over pointwise pressure/flow division.

Evaluation must include leave-one-brew-out, leave-one-condition-out, blocked
time prediction, condition-balanced aggregation, complexity and parameter
disclosure, low-flow/preinfusion/smoothing sensitivity, coverage/failure rates,
and uncertainty-aware comparisons. Use identical observations/windows, nested
selection, simple-model preference within uncertainty, and no complete-held-out-
brew fitting presented as prediction.

- Positive: one bounded evolving form has stable grouped predictive advantage;
  authorize only the smallest EWP-compatible component task.
- Negative: fixed is competitive or superior; preserve hydraulic simplicity and
  constrain uncertainty/ranges.
- Null: indistinguishable; retain fixed and quantify detectable effect.
- Blocked: qualify source observation semantics once; do not default to home-lab
  collection.

Maximum claim: source-internal controlled component evidence on the Waszkiewicz
apparatus that fixed or bounded evolving effective resistance better represents
the qualified basket-node pressure–flow–mass response. This does not establish
intrinsic permeability, universal transfer, whole-model validation, channeling,
compaction, fines migration, fracture, or grinder-to-cup validation. Visualizer
supplies future population envelopes; Wadsworth/Vaca may later supply separate
priors/operators. No home-lab work is needed and no scientific execution occurs
in C1.
