## Declared reduction and earliest completion

First examine the effective Eq. (11) on a compression-positive branch, not the
entire stochastic spring model. At fixed geometry, assume
`tau=A(g)P`, `sigma_c=sigma_0(g)+B(g)P`, with common pressure-independent
material/traction coefficients, `P>=0`, `C_E>=0` and
`D=c+sigma_0 tan(psi)>=0`. Low-Reynolds linear hydraulics motivates, but the
source yield law does not supply, this traction closure.

Then attempt `dx/dt=F(x,P,t)`, `q=K(x)P`: one scalar opening state, a
nonnegative mass-to-opening conversion, common initial state/material/time,
unique solutions on an invariant reachable interval, `F` nondecreasing in
pressure at fixed state/time, and nonnegative nondecreasing conductance.
Include the accepted predicted-flow line-to-bed adapter throughout evolution,
restricted to its nonnegative-flow branch. No assumption `F_x>=0` is required.
Finite cohesion/offset remains allowed. Full spatial/stochastic evolution is
separately unresolved unless a justified ordering argument is immediately
available.

**Earliest sufficient completion:** a rigorous scalar no-turnover theorem,
bounded source/observation applicability assessment, and independent review.
Do not search for a synthetic escape or run a parameter campaign after that
result. A small deterministic check may examine adapter algebra, history
ordering or processing support; no evolution calculation is planned.
