# SCI-LC-001A execution and integration rules

One scoped mutation lock controls repository, GitHub, readiness, and execution changes. The family controller is the only canonical launch route and checks authority, attempt ordinal, family hold, process identity, and per-key dispatch permission.

Historical E3 and E4 Attempts 01–03 are read-only and cannot be resumed, replayed, imported, reused, or combined. Attempt 04 must use a fresh empty root, exact merged protocol identity, `REAL_STAGE_A`, required diagnostics, and one supervised controller. Readiness is synthetic and cannot reach the canonical dispatcher.

Attempt 04 is terminal whether complete or ineligible. On failure the controller stops dispatch, finalizes the lease, preserves exact counts, quarantines the root, and publishes no classification. No automatic retry exists. Attempt 05 authority is `NONE`.

Classification is fail closed and may be atomically installed only from a complete, duplicate-free, lineage-valid, diagnostic-healthy Attempt 04 corpus. OpenFOAM, Puckworks, D4, X1, and alternate initial conditions are disabled.
