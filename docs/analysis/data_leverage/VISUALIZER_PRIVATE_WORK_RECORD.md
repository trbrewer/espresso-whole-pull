## Why this record exists

A substantial programme using a permissioned private Visualizer.coffee corpus
was intentionally retained outside Git to protect source privacy. It included
semantic preflight, reduced empirical reconstruction, corpus-scale comparison,
production EWP execution, onset/storage diagnosis, measurement-route design,
and machine-context assessment. The repository nevertheless needs a durable
account of its existence and the scientific and engineering decisions it
informed, including the general pressure-history capability added to EWP.

Future humans, coding agents, and reviewers must not interpret the absence of
private artifacts from Git as absence of prior analysis. The early blocked
boundary review was not the end of this history: later private work tested
empirical pressure-to-mass response and ran the production solver. This record
preserves those outcomes and their limits so ordinary task selection can avoid
unnecessary repetition while maintaining the privacy boundary. It records
completed work, changes no scientific disposition, and authorizes no new study.

## Durable findings

1. The corpus is useful for descriptive and empirical hydraulic analysis.

2. Recorded pressure is an empirical Visualizer signal; its physical sensor
   location and transfer to puck-face pressure remain unresolved.

3. Reduced pressure-driven models can reconstruct many individual mass curves,
   but cross-shot value is cohort-specific and missing shot-specific state is
   material.

4. A fair elapsed-time-and-dose baseline is often competitive with or better
   than shared pressure-driven prediction; post-hoc completed-duration
   baselines must not be treated as prospective predictors.

5. The production EWP solver can now consume piecewise-linear pressure
   histories and executed the bounded private comparison stably and
   conservatively.

6. Under the tested shared case, full EWP did not show clear no-retuning
   transfer value over the reduced dynamic model and predicted outlet onset too
   late.

7. Wetting/onset storage assumptions are material, but one common effective
   prehistory does not explain all tested cases.

8. Public machine specifications and market averages cannot supply the missing
   shot-specific machine identity, active storage, pressure transfer,
   calibration, or telemetry latency.

9. Further progress on the exhausted Visualizer boundary/onset decision
   requires new semantic authority or new synchronized measurements.

10. Physical validation remains `NOT_ESTABLISHED`.

## Public production consequence

XSV-PRESSURE-001 added the native piecewise-linear prescribed-pressure-history
interface to `espressoWholePullFoam`. The initial private five-shot exercise,
VIS-EWP-PLAY-003, stopped as
`VIS_EWP_PLAY_003_STOP_TRANSIENT_PRESSURE_INTERFACE_UNAVAILABLE`: the production
solver lacked a consistent tabulated pressure-history boundary. The interface
was subsequently implemented and qualified because that exercise exposed the
missing capability. Its public merge is
`73e3836f0ecdf14be61f504a5c7c7ae2019a250c`.

The [production interface documentation](../../PRESCRIBED_PRESSURE_HISTORY.md)
describes a general solver capability for supplied pressure histories. It
contains no Visualizer-specific parsing, changes no governing equation, and
does not validate Visualizer pressure as puck-face pressure. Converting an
external signal into a supported input format does not establish its physical
measurement boundary. No other private fit or parameter was adopted as a
production default. This preservation task itself changes neither the
interface nor any production behavior.

## What this record does not establish

The programme establishes no population representativeness and no exact machine
identity for modeled records. It establishes no pressure-sensor equivalence or
puck-face-pressure equivalence, and supports no physical permeability,
porosity, wetting, compaction, storage, or saturation inference. Effective
reconstruction quantities are not measurements of those properties. Internal
solver fields describe the tested mathematical case; they do not independently
observe the physical puck.

There is no independent validation, owner-apparatus qualification, Stage F or
Stage D authorization, permission to publish raw Visualizer data, or automatic
successor task. The [repository claim ceiling](../../CLAIM_CEILING.md) continues
to apply. Successful numerical execution and conservation do not remove
uncertain input semantics, while the bounded transfer result does not claim
that every possible EWP configuration has been tested. Post-hoc onset diagnosis
is not a prospective prediction or proof of an identified physical mechanism.

## Current decision

`EXISTING_VISUALIZER_DATA_LANE_COMPLETE_FOR_CURRENT_BOUNDARY_AND_ONSET_DECISIONS`

The corpus remains potentially useful for new questions. The named
pressure-boundary, generic transfer, onset/storage, and machine-prior questions
have been exercised to their current information limit. Another sweep using
the same inputs and assumptions should not be selected by default. More
simulation with those uncertain boundaries cannot identify the missing state
merely by increasing the number of cases or replacing absent identity with
market averages.

This is exhaustion for named decisions, not a declaration that the corpus has
no further scientific value. The programme progressed beyond an access or
interface blocker to completed empirical and production comparisons, followed
by targeted diagnosis and a machine-context check. Retaining that distinction
prevents a future task from presenting already exercised questions as undone,
without extending the findings beyond their tested scope.

## Revisit triggers

1. Explicit machine, parser, controller, sensor-location, flow-channel, or
   water-channel semantics for a relevant cohort.

2. Synchronized known-location pressure, cumulative inlet-water, cup-mass, and
   independent first-liquid measurements.

3. A materially new independent dataset with known apparatus and declared
   measurement boundaries.

4. A new scientific question that is demonstrably not equivalent to the
   exhausted pressure-boundary/onset questions.

5. Explicit owner authorization defining the new decision that the work could
   change.

Market averages alone are not a revisit trigger.

## Private evidence retention

Complete private reports, result files, traces, figures, run directories, and
manifests remain in owner-controlled storage outside Git. This repository
intentionally does not contain or link to local absolute paths. The public
record retains aggregate scope and durable decisions, rather than selected
curves, per-shot scores, cohort membership, or private case parameters.

Future maintainers should ask the owner for the private evidence package when
exact reproduction or audit is genuinely required. Unavailable private data
must not be treated as nonexistent data. This public-safe record is sufficient
for ordinary task selection and avoidance of duplicate work; public readers
are not being asked to treat it as a publicly reproducible evidence package.
Access to private evidence and any future execution remain separate decisions.
