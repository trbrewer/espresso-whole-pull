# SCI-MD-009 pilot design

Minimum: one homogenized lot, low/high flow, four replicates each, six mass-defined fractions, paired initial and spent-puck I_ref, absolute caffeine/trigonelline fraction masses, endpoint and telemetry (O6). Robust: low/middle/high flow, five replicates each and O7 metadata. Q_s is estimated and never fixed to one. Designs with one condition, fewer than four replicates, fewer than six fractions, or no spent-puck pairing are nonviable.
